## Introduction

Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers and A2 Flyers are designed specifically for young learners aged 6–12. They are fun, colourful and activity based, motivating children to learn. All three of the qualifications are aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) published by the Council of Europe, and demonstrate real-life communication skills.

### About these wordlists

At all levels (Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers and A2 Flyers) candidates need to be able to understand the 
words on the wordlist for their test. 
For more information about the exams go to: 
cambridgeenglish.org/starters 
cambridgeenglish.org/movers 
cambridgeenglish.org/flyers
